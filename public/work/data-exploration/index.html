01 / Context

What does the summary actually count?

In COS30045, I used KNIME to explore a television dataset: importing records, preparing fields, filtering, grouping by brand, and exporting a summary for visualisation.

The exported file contains Brand_Reg and Count(SoldIn). The numbers below are record counts from that export. They do not measure sales, popularity, or market share.

02 / Process

A workflow worth questioning.

  1. ImportRead the television data into a repeatable KNIME workflow.
  2. Prepare & filterExplore string cleaning and filters for availability and market.
  3. Group & exportAggregate records by brand label and export a CSV summary.
  4. Inspect againCheck whether different spellings split what appears to be one category.

The original export still contains uppercase, title-case, and lowercase variants. Keeping that limitation visible makes the next step in the analysis concrete.

04 / What the data reveals

Cleaning changes the question you can answer.

The export has three Kogan labels: KOGAN (246), Kogan (427), and kogan (115). Combining only their letter-case variants gives 788 records.

This is a useful reminder that a chart can accurately display a table and still give an incomplete picture. Labels, grouping decisions, and the unit being counted all need attention before the chart is interpreted.

Limit: This interactive view is a new presentation of the committed export, not a re-execution of the KNIME workflow. Case normalisation alone does not establish that all entities have been correctly resolved.
